ARBITER workflow and evaluation

ARBITER makes the basis of a Cochrane RoB 2 risk-of-bias assessment available for inspection: trial sources, signalling answers, cited evidence and rule-derived judgments.

This study compared the complete workflow with minimal prompting using the same reported model identifier and supplied documents for 102 trials from 16 reviews. ARBITER agreed more often with Human Review 1 and Human Review 2, without a clear agreement advantage against Published labels. Findings varied across domains and references. Structural record checks and judgment agreement are evaluated separately.
