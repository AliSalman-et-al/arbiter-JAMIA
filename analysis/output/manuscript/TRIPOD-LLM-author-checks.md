# Manuscript reporting checks

The checklist includes the author-supplied ethics, funding and conflict declarations. These declarations have not been inserted into the manuscript.

The following items remain partial or unreported in the current manuscript. They are described explicitly in the completed checklist.

| Item | Topic | Specific reporting gap |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | The task and randomized-trial setting are identified. The title does not explicitly identify an LLM performance evaluation. |
| 2 | Abstract | Background, paired design, endpoints, results and conclusions are reported. The abstract does not name or version the model, as requested by abstract item 2f. |
| 5a | Data sources | Evaluation sources and nonbenchmark workflow-development data are described. The base-model training corpus is not stated. |
| 5b | Data distribution | Review specialties, document availability and rating distributions are reported. Dataset language and country distributions are not described. |
| 5c | Text dates | Oldest and newest text dates for development and evaluation data are not stated. Review publication years do not establish these ranges. |
| 6a | Model identity | The model identifier, reasoning effort and host version are stated. The last training date and an immutable model-snapshot identifier are not stated. |
| 6c | Inference settings | Reasoning effort, host version, isolated settings and one run per arm per trial are stated. Temperature, output-length limits and an inference seed are not reported. |
| 7a | Generated-output quality | Rating and answer agreement and structural checks are reported. The relevance or support of generated rationales was not independently assessed. |
| 7c | Outcomes and inference dates | Targets, rating derivation and metrics are defined. Closed-source model inference dates are not stated in the manuscript or supplement. |
| 7d | Assessors | Two researchers, RoB 2 guidance, blinding and agreement are described. Professional qualifications, prior experience and calibration are not stated. |
| 8a | Annotation guidance | Assigned targets, RoB 2 guidance and freezing before label exposure are described. Reviewer-specific annotation instructions and worked annotation examples are not supplied. |
| 8c | Annotator experience | The assessors are described as two researchers. Their relevant professional background and RoB 2 experience are not stated. |
| 9a | Prompt design | Workflow instructions and prompt templates are supplied. Prompt curation, selection and version-freeze procedures are not fully described. |
| 13 | Ethics and consent | The authors deemed ethics approval unnecessary because publicly available data were used. Consent handling is not separately stated. |
| 19a | Interpretation | Main findings are interpreted against prior work and reference disagreement. Fairness is not explicitly discussed. |
| 19f | User expertise | Reviewer actions are specified. Minimum user training, calibration or a competence threshold is not stated. |

The official downloadable 2025 form enumerates the item identifiers used here. Healthcare-setting and individual-patient items are marked not applicable with reasons; this does not assert protocol, registration or public-involvement facts that the authors have not supplied.
