# Legends and alt text: Figures 1–2

**Figure 1. How ARBITER works.** The reviewer chooses a trial and outcome and approves the result to assess through `arbiter review`. The AI model (LLM agent) follows the ARBITER assessment guide, reads reports and registry records, and supplies answers, justifications and quotations. ARBITER is rule-based software with no AI: it stores source records, checks quotations, applies the official RoB 2 rules, and packages the `.arbiter.zip` audit file. Plain action labels are primary; small grey monospace labels identify representative tools. Across 102 trials, 53 of 637 `save_domain_judgment` calls were rejected (32 for evidence/citation structure, 21 for other input errors); every affected domain was saved after resubmission. Audit files retain source identities and evidence quotations, rather than complete trial PDFs, with answers and decision history. The assessing model can record a reasoned override; none occurred here.

Alt text: Four lanes separate Reviewer, lavender AI model, mint rule-based ARBITER server, and yellow Audit file across Capture, Propose and approve, Assess each domain, and Review and export. Trial reports and registry point to the top of Capture without touching the header. The reviewer chooses and approves; the model reads and answers; the server stores, checks and applies rules; the reviewer receives the audit file, checkable by an independent verifier.

The trial is illustrative, not representative. Each row connects a paraphrased signalling question, every cited snippet for that question, the AI answer and a plain-language summary of its recorded justification, ARBITER's quotation checks and complete answer pattern, and the computed result. Letter/page tabs link snippets to these cards. Each six-character fingerprint is the beginning of the quote's `source_version`, the stored text-copy identity, rather than the original PDF's identity. Snippets preserve source words, with ellipses for omissions and normalized extraction whitespace; extraction breaks in assignment and censored are joined.
