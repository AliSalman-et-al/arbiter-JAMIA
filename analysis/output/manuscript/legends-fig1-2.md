# Legends and alt text for Figures 1 and 2

**Figure 1. ARBITER assessment workflow.** The language-model agent finds evidence and answers questions. Software binds selected text to captured documents, applies RoB 2 algorithms and exports the assessment record. Binding locates text; it does not establish support for an answer. The audit file contains source identifiers and selected evidence, not complete documents. Reviewer approval is part of the general workflow but was not tested in the benchmark. AI indicates artificial intelligence; MCP, Model Context Protocol; RoB 2, the Cochrane risk-of-bias tool.

Alt text: A language-model agent searches captured trial documents, records answers and selects evidence. Software binds selected text to the captured extraction, applies RoB 2 algorithms and exports an assessment record. Reviewer approval belongs to the general workflow and was not tested in the benchmark. The audit file contains source identifiers and selected evidence, not complete source files. Binding locates text and does not establish that it supports an answer.

**Figure 2. AVATAR assessment linking evidence, answers and ratings.** AVATAR compares early surgery with watchful waiting for asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis; the outcome is all-cause death. The example illustrates the workflow, not typical performance. Its rationales were not independently validated. Letter/page tabs identify sources; counterevidence and unknowns remain visible. A No information answer about the dated analysis plan contributes to Some concerns overall. Footer labels concern D5, not independently rated Overall judgments. The simplified Overall rule omits permitted escalation to High when multiple Some concerns domains substantially lower confidence. The displayed P value does not establish adequate randomization. Y indicates yes; PY, probably yes; PN, probably no; N, no; NI, no information; D5, selection of the reported result; AI, artificial intelligence; RoB 2, the Cochrane risk-of-bias tool.

Alt text: AVATAR's five domains link source excerpts, model answers, software checks and ratings. Four domains are Low. A No information answer about the dated analysis plan contributes to Some concerns for selection of the reported result and Overall. Counterevidence and unknowns remain visible. The figure illustrates a model assessment; its rationales were not independently validated. The simplified Overall rule does not show every permitted escalation.
