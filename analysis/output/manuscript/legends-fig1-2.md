# Legends and alt text: Figures 1–2

**Figure 1. How ARBITER works.** The reviewer chooses a trial and outcome and approves the result to assess through `arbiter review`. The AI model (LLM agent) follows the ARBITER assessment guide, reads reports and registry records, and supplies answers, justifications and quotations. ARBITER is rule-based software with no AI: it stores source records, checks quotations, applies the official RoB 2 rules, packages the `.arbiter.zip` audit file, and writes the HTML report through `arbiter report`. Plain action labels are primary; small grey monospace labels identify representative tools. Across 102 trials, 53 of 637 `save_domain_judgment` calls were rejected (32 for evidence/citation structure, 21 for other input errors); every affected domain was saved after resubmission. Audit files retain source identities and evidence quotations, rather than complete trial PDFs, with answers and decision history. The assessing model can record a reasoned override; none occurred here.
