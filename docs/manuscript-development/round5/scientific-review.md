## Checks determining the disposition

- **Reporting order and chronology:** manuscript lines 85–91 and Results lines 107–111 put Published-label exact agreement first, independent expert exact agreement next, and conditional blinded matching afterward. Line 85 explicitly says the reporting order was adopted after analysis. No arbitrary outcome hierarchy or prospective claim is reinstated. Exploratory and descriptive qualifications remain where they describe the analyses rather than manufacture a confirmatory hierarchy.
- **Denominators and uncertainty:** all 510 domain cells remain distinct from 80 arm disagreements, 60 agreed choices and 20 splits. The 53/60 result is conditional label equality, with the individual 63/80 and 61/80 results and 53/80 split-as-loss sensitivity retained. Signalling-answer component denominators, the 509 evaluable Unassisted domain outputs, and the 81/102 Published Overall population remain separate. Lines 103 and 135 restrict CIs to trial variation within fixed reviews, conditional on the observed experts and runs; line 125 avoids interpreting individual intervals as tests between domains.
- **Unfavorable evidence:** the negative Published-label difference and all-Low prevalence comparator remain, as do the adverse five-level D3 contrast, expert disagreement, rule breaks and unevaluable output, wrong-trial De Waal registry input, greater computation, unknown immutable snapshot equivalence and inability to attribute effects to components. Neither collapsed coding nor the conditional-choice result is used to erase those findings.
- **Mechanism and semantic correctness:** lines 55–61 distinguish target approval, captured-source text selection, model-written visual transcription, rule-derived proposals, adopted ratings and structural audit. They do not claim that source identity establishes the correct trial, that authentic text is relevant or complete, that image transcription is verified, or that conformance proves clinical correctness. The study audit is explicitly distinguished from independent quotation re-extraction. Lines 117 and 129 distinguish algorithmic proposal generation from observed adopted-rating conformance and absence of domain overrides.
- **Author-supported facts and remaining uncertainty:** lines 67–79 attribute selection and exclusion appropriately, leave Atwine's subtype unverified, and do not invent search/ranking dates or certify highest-ranked selection. Development material is identified as ten prostate-cancer trials plus synthetic cases; benchmark non-use is attributed to the author without an exhaustive-exposure claim. Experts are described as published BEACON researchers given matched trial materials, target prompts and Cochrane guidance. Individual qualifications, experience, calibration, multi-outcome conventions, blank form, complete development/selection history and version-freeze documentation remain visibly pending. Back-matter declarations remain placeholders rather than asserted approvals.
- **Literature and clinical register:** the Introduction establishes result-specific appraisal before introducing ARBITER. Prior work is synthesized by capability and evaluative boundary, without transferring EviSearch/FD-SCoPE task misattributions or claiming a uniquely verified novelty from the publication-history sample. The new Riaz reference is a perspective supporting motivation, not evidence of ARBITER time savings or reviewer benefit. ROBOTO2's conservative/no-information tendency is source-supported. Gandhi and Huang support the distinction between review-derived and newly collected human references; Liang supports the separate judge-model evidence-support task. No inspected passage contradicts these uses. Terminology is suitable for evidence-synthesis and medical-informatics readers, and the conclusion stays within workflow traceability and this benchmark's reference-dependent agreement.

These checks identify no required prose correction. Existing visible placeholders remain author-completion work, not newly discovered defects or grounds for inventing facts.
