# Candidate A rationale

## Principal structural choice

The argument moves from the evaluation of a specified implementation to the limits of its observed agreement advantage. The Introduction establishes the interpretation/rule-application distinction briefly, synthesizes existing systems and prompt comparisons, then explains why holding the model and supplied documents constant makes this bundled workflow contrast informative. It does not manufacture a field-wide absence of evidence-linked or rule-constrained assessment.

The Discussion opens with the overall interpretation but immediately states the conditional primary endpoint and its denominator. Independent agreement is explicitly secondary. Domain and reference dependence follow before the account of the implementation, so conformance cannot become the explanation for an unqualified performance claim. Subsequent paragraphs distinguish conformance, source binding, semantic support, component attribution, generalization and reviewer use without reintroducing the entire conceptual framework each time.

The title names both the inspectable workflow and its actual comparator. The five-part abstract retains the two expert-specific absolute comparisons and paired estimates, Published contrast, rule result and runtime. Prose counts excluding headings and citation markers are approximately 216 words for the abstract and 1,150 for Introduction plus Discussion. These counts concern this narrative deliverable, not the integrated article.

## Alternatives rejected

- A tutorial opening defining risk of bias or the five domains would repeat knowledge held by the intended readers. The opening instead names the study's interpretive problem.
- A novelty argument organized around the first evidence-linked or rule-constrained system would conflict with the source record. Prior systems delimit the question rather than supply a foil.
- Leading the Discussion solely with the all-assessment secondary findings could obscure the formal primary endpoint. Leading solely with 88% could encourage an all-assessment accuracy reading. The first paragraph gives the interpretation, conditional denominator and endpoint boundary together.
- A component-led explanation of the agreement gains would exceed the complete-workflow design. Rules-only recomputation is retained as a consistency analysis rather than a component experiment.
- Repeating every Results count would crowd out interpretation. The D3 coding reversal, smaller D5 contrast and Published contrast earn numerical detail because they materially qualify the main claim. Individual-choice and split-loss sensitivities remain in the untouched baseline Results; this narrative does not replace or delete them.
- An AVATAR retelling would duplicate the system illustration already retained in Methods. The Discussion instead explains the inspectable operations directly. The De Waal source-identity caution remains because it bears on shared-input interpretation.
