## Required repairs and integration checks

1. **Replace the model-control assertion in either candidate.** Suggested replacement for B's third Introduction paragraph: “We compared the complete workflows using the same reported model identifier and configuration and identical supplied trial documents. Instructions, tools and processing differed; the arms ran on different dates, and identity of the underlying model snapshot was not established.” The reported identifier is `claude-sonnet-5-5`, medium effort; ARBITER ran on 12 October and Unassisted on 14 October 2026. A shared identifier supports the descriptive same-model framing, but does not prove a fixed snapshot or remove all temporal uncertainty. Do not invent evidence that the snapshot changed either. Carry the qualification into the comparator limitation if the integrated Methods does not already make it explicit.

2. **Make the access relationship explicit.** B's “Our experts could consult sources beyond the arms' inputs” correctly describes expert-versus-arm access. A's “unequal access” could imply unequal permissions between Expert 1 and Expert 2, which the canonical text does not establish. If any of A's reference paragraph is retained, replace that phrase with “Experts could consult sources beyond the documents supplied to either arm.” Do not attribute the D3 disagreement to additional sources, training, calibration or judgment thresholds.

For a title readable outside the specialist RoB literature, use “ARBITER: an inspectable risk-of-bias workflow compared with same-model minimal prompting”; retain the full RoB 2 name in B's abstract. The same-model descriptor needs the snapshot qualification in the body. Keep B's concise reliability context: it supplies a reason to examine the human reference without teaching readers what risk of bias means. Do not restore definitions of all five domains or tokens simply for accessibility.

Retain the official section structure around these narrative replacements. Resolve the three new literature markers into complete references and renumber citations only at integration.
