# Manuscript development

## Definition of done

A revised JAMIA Research and Applications manuscript has a coherent, bounded argument; its study claims trace to finalized data; cited literature supports the attached claims; and HTML and editable JAMA-styled Word versions derive from the same source. Pages navigation and downloads resolve. Word and browser artifacts are inspected. Statistical exports and protected Figures 1 and 2 remain unchanged. Independent review findings are resolved or explicitly recorded. Author-only declarations remain visibly pending when not supplied.

## Scope and workflow

1. Ground. Audit the existing manuscript, analysis plan, glossary, outputs and publishing pipeline. Read the clinical writing skill and relevant passages in Schimel, Heard and Zinsser. Capture baseline checks and hashes.
2. Frame and sketch. Compare two story structures and publishing designs. Preserve the formal primary endpoint and distinguish full-cohort agreement, process checks and conditional preference. Record a story contract and source ownership.
3. Research swarm. Luna Medium checks journal requirements and primary literature. Sol 6.1 Medium builds the writing rubric and competing outlines. Reports have separate owned paths. Aggregate by coverage, not voting.
4. Draft. Sol 6.1 Medium revises the manuscript against the accepted contract. Senior-author review examines structure, clarity, flow, claims and citations before line polish.
5. Publish. Extend the existing source-to-Word pipeline with a manuscript HTML page. Preserve finalized analyses and existing publication styling.
6. Review and refine. Independent scientific and editorial reviews, numerical and citation checks, browser inspection and Word page proofs. Reflect on workflow failures using a session digest; do not change shared skills without the approval required by reflect.
7. Verify and hand back. Commit verified units, publish the approved-scope Pages artifacts, and distinguish completed scientific writing from pending author declarations.
