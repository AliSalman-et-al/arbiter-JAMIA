# Mechanism and evaluation sequence: proposed revision

Status: author directions recorded; mechanism verification and independent full-text dissection in progress. Not a replacement manuscript or a changed analysis plan.

## Story

Opening: RoB 2 assessment is necessary for interpreting trial evidence and requires repeated, result-specific judgments. Maintaining current evidence syntheses creates a reason to investigate automation. Support workload claims with direct sources rather than assuming that automation saves reviewer time.

Challenge: AI can assist appraisal, but plausible labels and supporting quotations do not by themselves establish a dependable assessment. The reader must be able to distinguish evidence selection and interpretation from application of the decision rules, and assess performance against clearly described references. Existing systems already address parts of this problem; explain their contributions and remaining limitations specifically.

Action: introduce Agentic Risk of Bias Inference Tool for Evidence Reviews (ARBITER), explain the operational sequence and what each safeguard controls, then describe the complete-workflow benchmark and its reference comparisons.

Resolution: return to what the study establishes about a retained assessment record and observed agreement, with the unfavorable Published comparison and conditional primary endpoint explicit. Living evidence is a motivating application, not an evaluated deployment.

## System description sequence

1. Specify the result and applicable RoB 2 assessment.
2. Retain the supplied/acquired sources and identify the exact document versions.
3. Select textual evidence through document tools; explain what arguments the model supplies and what text the software extracts, once verified against the evaluated implementation.
4. Link signalling answers to retained evidence. Distinguish source text, image records and model-written descriptions.
5. Apply the official algorithms to the answers to obtain proposed ratings; describe the separate adopted-rating and justified-override mechanism.
6. Export the source-linked assessment for inspection. State the human role in interactive use and the automated benchmark's different handling of approval.

For each step: operation -> reason for that operation -> narrow boundary where material. Do not repeatedly append generic disclaimers. The text-extraction safeguard concerns fabricated quotation wording; it does not establish evidence relevance or valid inference.

## Evaluation order

Confirmed author requirement: Published-label agreement precedes independent-expert agreement. Exact agreement precedes chance-adjusted agreement and other statistics within each relevant comparison.

Proposed Results sequence: benchmark composition and reference distributions -> exact agreement with Published -> exact agreement with each independent expert and paired contrasts -> conditional blinded-label-choice primary endpoint and its sensitivities -> domain/signalling-answer detail -> rule and source-record audits -> computation. The independent expert procedure must still describe ratings frozen before exposure to offered labels. Formal primary designation remains unchanged and explicit in the abstract, Objectives and Outcomes regardless of narrative position.

Match Outcomes subsections to this sequence where useful, with an opening statement of the formal hierarchy. Assess figure/table first-call order after rearranging; a narrative change must not silently leave displays out of sequence. Preserve all numerical values, populations and unfavorable results.

This is a logical sequence for the reader, not a claim that the study or analyses were planned in this chronological order. The original design history remains an open author question.
