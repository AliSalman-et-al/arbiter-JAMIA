## Editorial rationale

The formal primary endpoint retains its designation, population and scoring; the all-cell independent expert comparisons explain the broader agreement findings. The primary denominator pathway remains adjacent to the result: 80 arm disagreements, 60 agreed choices and 20 splits, followed by 53/60 and all three core sensitivity results. Label-choice match replaces language implying that experts selected a system or its reasoning. Statistical terminology appropriate for medical researchers is retained. Sentence lengths vary with the information they carry; the prose uses neither tutorial pronouns nor an arbitrary short-sentence limit. Results retain the negative five-level D3 finding, its reversal under collapsed coding, the small D5 contrast against Expert 2, the Published-reference contrast and computational burden. No causal explanation or between-domain significance claim is added.

The revision follows the inspected clinical-research-writing `SKILL.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, `references/revision-and-coaching.md`, `references/manuscript-workflow.md` and `references/statistics-and-claims.md` under `/home/ali/Documents/Code/skills/clinical-research-writing/`. ## Before/after terminology map

- “Content-hashed sources” becomes “source documents with digital fingerprints that identify their exact contents.” This preserves the exact-version function of the hash.
- “Binds evidence records to source locations” becomes “extracts quoted passages and records their locations.” Later audit wording specifies links to the captured source and its location.
- “Server-extracted spans” becomes “software extracts text quotations directly from the stored documents.” The actor and operation are explicit.
- “A reviewer approves” becomes a distinction between reviewer approval in interactive use and unchanged acceptance by the automated benchmark procedure.
- “Harness” becomes “automated run procedure.” Its approval is not presented as independent human review.
- “Registry projections” becomes “stored copies of registry records.” The comparison still uses captured material rather than a fresh fetch.
- “Assessment skill” is introduced as a package of assessment instructions and guidance; its reproducibility name remains available.
- “ARBITER preference” and “favored ARBITER” become “agreed expert label choices matching ARBITER.” Credit depends on label equality, including shared labels.
- “Component” is defined as an explicitly named part of a signalling answer; an undivided answer has one unnamed component. The matching rule is explained separately from domain-rating analysis.
- “Invalid paths” becomes “invalid answer sequences.” Required-question omissions and the sensitivity analysis remain explicit.
- “Structural evidence binding” becomes checks linking evidence records to their stored sources. Passing those checks is not described as proof that the evidence supports an answer.
- “Bundles” becomes “audit files” in the main prose. These are retained assessment packages, not counts of answers or quotations.
- “Wall-clock turn durations,” “token components” and “side calls” become elapsed model-turn durations, recorded token counts and additional model calls. API is expanded once, and tokens are defined.

## Sentences requiring supplement or source correction

1. **“Excluding three trials recorded as having unsupported designs left 102 trials.”** `ANALYSIS-PLAN.md` names Atwine 2020, Haney 2004 and Herrmann 2016, and `analysis/R/load.R` excludes all three. Direct inspection of their stored `canonical.json` records found `unsupported_design` and crossover applicability for Haney and Herrmann, but `assessed` for Atwine. The candidate preserves the approved 102-trial population and adds a pending field. Confirm Atwine's exclusion rationale and the design-specific eligibility account before submission; do not infer that all three bundles themselves rejected the designs. No population change was made.

2. **“eMethods reproduces both prompt templates, the command invoking the ARBITER assessment skill, the Unassisted format instruction and a filled example.”** The historical runner and README use `/rob2-assess`; `analysis/R/materials.R` and the generated `prompt-template-example.csv` use `/arbiter-assess`. The supplement should distinguish the verbatim evaluated command from the public renamed command and document their correspondence. Final templates and a filled example do not complete prompt-development reporting. TRIPOD-LLM items 9a–9b should remain partial until design/selection, development data and freeze provenance are documented. These sources were not edited.

3. **“eMethods summarizes the assessment sequence; the completed assessment workbooks are available in the study repository.”** This replaces the false assertion that eMethods supplies the assessment form. The sequence is present, but no original blank form or assessor instructions were located among the inspected assessment records. Add those materials and a precise locator when supplied. Guidance quotations in completed answers do not prove which instructions were given beforehand. Review-selection, assessor-experience and annotation-guidance reporting-map entries also need appropriately qualified status.

4. **The new definition of a signalling-answer component.** It is supported by the actual parser, but eMethods currently uses “component” without explaining the named/unnamed distinction. Add that definition to the supplement while leaving the assessor's multi-outcome judgment convention visibly pending. Do not add an invented rule for deriving the experts' recorded domain judgments.

5. **“The algorithm determines the proposed ratings; the absence of overrides and conformance of the adopted ratings were observations in this benchmark.”** This sentence clarifies the unchanged Table 3 wording. `main_table3()` substitutes “0 by design” for selected ARBITER rule-check rows, whereas the retained audit export separately reports observed zero rule breaks and zero overrides. A future table-note correction should identify the proposed/adopted distinction; this assignment does not authorize changing that table or its generator. An override permitted by the system is not inherently an erroneous judgment.

6. **“Reporting was guided by TRIPOD-LLM and GRRAS, with an accompanying item map and author-completion fields.”** This avoids claiming complete reporting. The map currently marks prompt-development items as reported on the strength of template locations. Revise those statuses when the parent performs the reporting-source corrections; do not silently convert missing information into negative assertions.

All existing author-only front- and back-matter fields remain outside this candidate and must remain pending in integration: author list, affiliations, correspondence, acknowledgments, funding, conflicts, contributions, ethics and consent, registration, public involvement, final AI-use confirmation and archival DOI/dataset citation. This report does not complete or remove them.
