# Senior-author grounding for revision

The intended reader knows trials, evidence synthesis and uncertainty. Explain the software operations, not elementary clinical research. Formal scientific prose should remain natural: sentence-length rules and developer-document conventions yield to accurate biomedical writing.

The central question is how an available RoB 2 workflow preserves the basis of an assessment and how its complete implementation compares with minimal prompting. Agreement with experts does not establish more defensible reasoning. This corrects the overbroad phrase in the older story-contract distillation while retaining that contract's intended system-led structure.

## Practices to use

Schimel's problem-before-solution principle: start with the need to examine why a trial result received a risk-of-bias judgment. Introduce ARBITER after explaining the gap this particular evaluation addresses. The tool's existence is not its own rationale.

Heard's results organization: keep the formal conditional result visible, then lead readers to the independent full-cohort comparisons that explain the agreement conclusion. Give each unfavorable pattern a clear paragraph job. Do not turn Results into a catalogue of every analysis.

Clinical-writing guidance: keep familiar concepts in sentence openings, then introduce the new operation. Preserve qualifications that change the claim. Simplification must not erase denominators, imperfect references, unequal prompting or the narrow scope of the bootstrap.

The inspected Riaz empirical paper on clinical outcome extraction (DOI 10.1038/s41746-025-01646-7) explains teacher/student operations beside the clinical task, defines the precise study question and states what the evaluation cannot replace. Apply those structural practices to model interpretation and software-applied rules. Do not copy sentences. Coauthored papers and invited reviews do not establish a single personal style. The Cancer review's broad future-facing rhetoric is less suitable for this bounded empirical paper and should not be imported.

Use a compact technical term after explaining it only when it earns repeated use. Keep standard statistical names such as bootstrap, weighted kappa and confidence interval. Define Unassisted as the minimal-prompt model comparator and preserve its exact identity in figures/tables. Do not imply a human-only comparator.

## Literature use

Synthesize prior work by what it establishes, not by author chronology. Cite closest systems for evidence display and review, conformance studies for the answer-to-rating distinction, and evidence-support work for the separate need to assess interpretation. Keep cross-study designs explicit where differences affect inference. Do not insert citations to the PI solely because he is the PI.

Preserve the current five-figure/three-table structure and all finalized statistical values. The table wording '0 by design' needs a clear proposed-versus-adopted-rating explanation in prose; display redesign is outside this revision.

Pending factual details cannot be polished into facts. Missing review-selection history, prompt-development history, assessor qualifications and declarations remain explicit until records support completion.
