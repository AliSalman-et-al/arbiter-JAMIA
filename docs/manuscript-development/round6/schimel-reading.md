# Direct Schimel reading and application

Source: /home/ali/Documents/Code/skills/book-references/Schimel Joshua/Writing Science_ How to Write Papers t Cited and Proposals That Get Funded/index.html. Parent inspected actual book text, not only skill summaries. Plain-text extraction /tmp/schimel-writing-science.txt preserves chapter headings; line locators below refer to that extraction.

- Chapter 3.3–3.4 (lines 301–318): abstract concepts need concrete grounding; credibility is a chain connecting prior evidence, methods, results and conclusions. Application: replace vague “studies show” with selected contextualized findings, not merely more citations.
- Chapter 5.3 (528–553): examples contrast specialist and generalist openings. Application: assume clinical appraisal expertise; explain unfamiliar computation without elementary RoB tutorials.
- Chapter 6.2–6.3 (656–711): define a precise gap; introduction is not an inventory of literature; findings usually deserve the sentence subject more than investigators. Application: each selected study must establish a boundary of knowledge relevant to ARBITER's question. Avoid sequential “X studied…Y studied…” prose unless the disagreement/design comparison itself matters.
- Chapter 7.2–7.4 (728 onward): question precedes task objectives; “to learn X, we did Y” connects purpose and method. Application: make the unknown explicit before enumerating evaluation outcomes.
- Chapter 8.2.2–8.3 (855–907): select results needed to develop the story, provide a pattern before detail, and use Discussion to interpret and answer the challenge. Application: preserve unfavorable results but remove redundant repetition of full counts/intervals when the Results already supplies them.
- Chapter 11 (1178–1251): paragraphs are complete local arguments; point-first development and point-last synthesis both legitimate. Application: claim → specific evidence → meaning/remaining boundary, with real transitions rather than bullet-like sentences.
- Chapter 16.10 (2438–2454): overcondensation can create cryptic writing; words that establish coherence are useful. Application: allow enough space for cited evidence and its implication; do not force every paragraph into short abstract statements.
- Chapter 17 (2458–2510): revise structure, clarity, flow, language iteratively. Application: choose a coherent base before sentence-level grafting.
- Chapter 18 opening and Methods (2538–2585): constrain claims to what the methods actually measure, address weaknesses openly. Application: integrate limitations into interpretation; do not let provenance narration displace the study's scientific contribution.

The book is a writing guide, not statistical authority: its illustrative p-value probability interpretation in §8.2.4 is not adopted. Modern claim discipline and the actual analysis govern inference. Source scope is selected relevant chapters/passages, not a claim of reading the entire book in this pass.
