# Parent and cross-judge rubric

Score each criterion 0–4. Scientific fidelity and source accuracy must each score 4 before acceptance, irrespective of total. Candidates receive the task/evidence contract, not this scoring sheet.

1. Scientific fidelity: preserves estimands, all interpretation-changing findings, author-confirmed methods and limits; no false prospective, causal, accuracy or utility claims.
2. Source accuracy and context: each imported finding/number matches the inspected source's metric, population, design and reference; preprint/correction status clear; no inappropriate cross-study numerical rankings.
3. Argument development: evidence advances a precise claim and knowledge gap; facts lead to bounded interpretation rather than a study catalogue.
4. Scientific manuscript voice: investigator-authored account and integrated interpretation, not an audit report, repository log or catalogue of disclaimers. Essential limitations remain readable and explicit.
5. Structure and flow: clear opening–challenge–action–resolution, coherent paragraph jobs and transitions, introduction's promise answered in discussion/conclusion.
6. Audience and economy: expert clinical register with concrete computational explanations, sufficient evidence development within journal limits, no filler or redundant qualifications.
