## What to retain and what to resist

Across the inspected confirmed articles, citations develop a problem or explanation: an established effect creates a narrower question; an inadequacy in prior analysis motivates a design; conflicting findings are interpreted through differences in measurement or design. That is the relevant article-level pattern. It supports richer reasoning between the claim and the caveat, not more ornamental citations and not a mandate for uniformly short sentences.

The CABG paper also supplies counterexamples. Its abstract and Discussion limitations say nineteen studies, while Discussion ¶1 says twenty. The overall ticagrelor mortality result is OR 1.04 (95% CI 0.65–1.65), whereas the post-CABG subgroup is OR 0.49 (0.33–0.73); the favorable subgroup cannot replace the overall comparison (Results → aspirin/ticagrelor versus aspirin/clopidogrel → All-cause mortality). Several Results paragraphs imply that funnel symmetry establishes robustness, and the Conclusion suggests that a large sample reduces residual confounding. Neither is a suitable inference to import into ARBITER. Preserve the explanation-rich structure while improving claim calibration.

The qualified palliative-care paper provides a further example: Introduction ¶4 (`palliative.xml`, `/article/body/sec[@id='s0005']/p[4]`) combines a guideline expectation with cited national utilization of 4% on average and change from 0.4% to 6.2% during 2002–2017, then interprets reactive referral as the unresolved practice problem. This is what makes evidence into motivation. However, the article's aim promises impact/outcomes while its limitations state that the data cannot assess clinical impact. Its home-health-discharge sensitivity definition is also broader than specialty consultation. These boundaries prevent using the paper as a model of validated effect inference, and its CRediT prevents personal prose attribution to Ali.

## Why the present draft feels like a report, and the manuscript-level response

The draft already has defensible distinctions and real findings. Its problem is the distribution of explanatory work. Introduction ¶2 says reliability studies document disagreement but gives no concrete finding; ¶3 then inventories several systems. Discussion ¶¶3, 5, 6 and 8 repeatedly name studies, classify their designs, and finish by restricting what can be concluded. Those are useful analytical notes, but the reader often learns more about claim boundaries than about the scientific understanding produced by ARBITER.

The strongest organizing question is: **What changes when trial-evidence interpretation remains with a model but the subsequent rating pathway becomes explicit and executable, and how does the observed agreement depend on the reference?** The benchmark can describe those changes at whole-workflow level. It cannot attribute a gain to an individual component. Make that restriction once where the comparison is interpreted, then develop the answer positively through the actual observations.

Recommended revisions for the parent, without drafting or altering canonical text:

- **Introduction:** develop a problem, evidence, implication and gap over connected paragraphs. Choose one verified RoB reliability result with population, assessment unit and finding from the existing reliability research; explain its consequence for appraisal. Then use selected prior-system findings to show which parts of the process have been evaluated and which relation remains uncertain. Do not insert cardiology findings into the ARBITER rationale merely to resemble this corpus.
- **End of Introduction:** state the unknown before the task list. Agreement against published labels and independent experts, together with the executable assessment record, addresses a specific uncertainty about evaluation and inspectability. A catalogue of outcomes alone does not give that reason.
- **Discussion opening:** answer the study question with the observed reference-dependent pattern. Develop why the contrast between published and fresh independent ratings changes interpretation. The present draft repeats the complete 80/60/20/53 conditional accounting after Results already supplies it; retain the necessary subset qualification while giving the explanatory argument more room.
- **Discussion middle:** organize around three propositions: the reference changes the assessment; executable rules settle the answer-to-rating step while leaving interpretation open; the retained evidence makes unresolved decisions inspectable. Use the relevant observed domain pattern or AVATAR trace to develop each proposition. Prior studies belong at the point where a design difference explains a result or defines the next unknown.
- **Methods and supplement:** keep indispensable design limitations in the main narrative, including purposive sampling, target correspondence, minimal prompting, one observed run and conditional intervals. Move recoverable operational provenance detail—command-name history, release-equivalence documentation and audit bookkeeping—to eMethods, while retaining direct pointers. Preserve unresolved author-completion information in the project record; do not invent it or let it dominate the scientific narrative.
- **Ending:** frame the next study around the specific unresolved link from evidence to answer and the time/behavior of reviewers checking it. End on the demonstrated contribution and the bounded question it enables, with limitations already integrated where they affect interpretation.

This follows the actual local book rather than a generic OCAR slogan. Schimel §6.2–6.3 distinguishes a concrete unanswered question from an inventory of knowledge; §11.1–11.2 permits both point-first development and evidence-led synthesis; §16.10 argues that connective explanation earns its space; §18.1–18.2 asks that promises and interpretation fit the methods. Direct reading used the local `index.html`, with a fresh extraction at `schimel-local-extract.txt` (chapter 6: lines 2249–2366; chapter 11: 4048–4180; §16.10: 7858 onward). The point is not to shorten the draft further. It is to give each paragraph enough linked evidence and reasoning to reach a scientific conclusion.
