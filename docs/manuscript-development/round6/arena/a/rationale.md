## Alternatives considered and rejected

1. **A broad automation/living-review opening.** Rejected because general review-production times and future automation frameworks would delay the actual appraisal problem. Minozzi's assessment unit, agreement and task time provide a more direct empirical opening. Riaz is not cited merely as an exemplar or courtesy citation.
2. **An inventory of contemporary systems.** Reduced to the prior capabilities needed to establish the question. RobotReviewer and ROBOTO2 establish that classifications, text and editable assessments already exist. The Discussion uses Gandhi, Choi and Liang for three different scientific propositions; their values are never ranked against ARBITER.
3. **Huang numeric algorithm-versus-direct comparison.** Omitted. The parent correction overrides the researcher reconstruction: each trial was assessed twice, so 11/20 and 19/20 are unsafe inferred counts. No inferred numerator or Huang performance percentage appears. The qualitative structured-prompt citation retains the corrected title, “Evaluation Study.”
4. **RoBGuard headline accuracy and RoB2-Evaluator performance.** Omitted: the former lacked a clear split/denominator for the proposed number, and the latter lacked inspected full text. No inaccessible result or superiority/novelty claim was substituted.
5. **A larger related-work numeric survey.** ROBOTO2 retrieval/edit rates, Nyrhi kappa, Bertizzolo older-tool disagreement and Jardim acceptance ratios were unnecessary to this argument. They would add distinct estimands without resolving an additional question. Jardim is retained qualitatively to complement the directly measured time study.
6. **Treating expert agreement as accuracy.** Rejected because experts disagreed, the references differed, and neither reference is error-free. The conditional blinded choices are label matches; source identities and rationales were not shown.
7. **Attributing the agreement difference to deterministic rules.** Rejected. Choi motivates evaluating internal consistency, while the present comparison remains a complete-workflow contrast. Rules-only recomputation cannot recover a component effect.
8. **Calling source checks semantic validation or human time savings.** Rejected. The mechanism stores actual captured slices after source selection; it does not validate extraction fidelity or interpretation. Soboczenski's modeled relative time is explicitly separated from its raw means, and neither transfers to ARBITER.
9. **Relocating all design limitations.** Rejected. Purposive and availability-dependent selection, unrecovered search/ranking records, target revision/correspondence, Atwine's author-designated unsupported status with unknown subtype, the unrelated De Waal registry, author-confirmed development independence, same expert inputs/task prompts, unknown immutable snapshot identity, two experts and one run remain in the main manuscript. Only operational provenance detail is proposed for supplementary consolidation.
