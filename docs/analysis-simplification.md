## Argument and review

Opening. Evidence reviewers need to inspect the evidence and decisions behind result-specific risk-of-bias judgments.

Challenge. An inspectable record does not establish defensible judgments, and human references can differ.

Action. Describe the model/software division and retained record, then compare complete workflows against Published labels and two independently recorded expert references.

Resolution. ARBITER preserved an inspectable pathway and agreed more often with both experts in this benchmark, without a clear Published-label agreement advantage. Evidence support, reviewer benefit and causal component effects remain untested.

Three independent reviewers defended every original display family and analysis variant. Scientific judgment and reader/argument reviews used GPT-6-Astra. Evidence/integrity review used GPT-6.1-Sol. A separate synthesis reconciled disagreements. These were content reviews against the manuscript, original outputs and earlier Git revision. They did not certify the modified pipeline or rendered publication.

## Restorations that changed the initial proposal

The original proposal cut too much diagnostic information. The independent reviews changed three decisions.

- Retain derived expert Overall. Original ARBITER agreement is 39/102 with Expert 1 and 72/102 with Expert 2; expert agreement is 52/102. This qualifies generalization from pooled domain agreement. It is not a directly rated sixth domain.
- Retain complete paired question results. Q4.1 five-level differences are unfavorable against both experts, with zero collapsed differences. Domain summaries alone conceal that distinction.
- Retain pooled rules-only repair. Original Unassisted agreement of 332/510 and 400/510 becomes 324/510 and 391/510 after repairing its 26 contradictory labels. This does not isolate a causal component effect.

The current numbered supplement has four families. They cover Published agreement, independent agreement/reliability, category context, and signalling/eligibility. Methods, prompts, provenance and a short linked-record index accompany them. Conditional choices are already in main Table 2.
