# loncol-cdk46

CDK4/6 inhibitors plus endocrine therapy: IPD meta of RCTs
doi: 10.1016/s1470-2045(26)00053-7

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: CDK4/6 inhibitor plus endocrine therapy vs placebo plus the same endocrine therapy (Table S3 'Comparison' column). Time-to-event outcomes, no fixed time point: PFS/OS/TFC/CFS/DOR from Kaplan-Meier curves reconstructed to IPD. Table S3 'Original reported HR' values show PFS taken from each trial's primary PFS report and OS from the later OS reports the meta cites (refs 6, 16, 18, 20, 21, 23, 25 in the manuscript). One RoB 2 row per trial (Figure S14), no outcome named anywhere (C1, C3, C4). Mixed-population trials: MONALEESA-3 and MONALEESA-7 split into endocrine-sensitive/resistant for the subgroup pools; MONARCH-plus cohorts A and B both used (C9). MONALEESA-7: whole trial (ribociclib plus tamoxifen or NSAI) for PFS, plus an NSAI-only OS row.
