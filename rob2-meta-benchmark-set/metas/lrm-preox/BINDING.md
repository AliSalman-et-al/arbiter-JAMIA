# lrm-preox

Preoxygenation strategies for intubation of critically ill patients: SR and NMA
doi: 10.1016/s2213-2600(25)00029-3

Binding: stated: rated per outcome (eTable 3 has one row per trial per outcome)

Contrast: Network meta-analysis with nodes HFNC, NIPPV, facemask (and NIPPV+HFNC for Jaber 2016). Mortality is 'longest endpoint reported' (S2). Per-trial contrasts as printed in eFigure 3 (mortality, physical p40) and eFigure 8 (first pass success, physical p45): Chua, Guitton, Rosen = HFNC vs Facemask; Baillard 2006, Baillard 2018 = NIPPV vs Facemask; Frat = HFNC vs NIPPV; Jaber = NIPPV vs NIPPV+HFNC. All seven trials are two-arm. eTable 3 rates each trial separately for Mortality, Peri-intubation (hypoxaemia), 1st pass success and Adverse events; the row set varies by trial. For all six mortality-labelled trials here the Peri-intubation row (and 1st pass / Adverse events rows where present) carries the same D1-D5 values as the Mortality row; for Rosen the 1st pass and Adverse events rows are identical. The meta main text could not be fetched (Lancet and ScienceDirect return 403, not open access in Europe PMC), so its methods and RoB section were not read; S1-S3 come from the supplement, S4 from the abstract.

Statements in the meta about what the RoB 2 ratings cover:

- S1: "We will rate the risk of bias at an outcome level. Two reviewers, working independently and in duplicate, will assess the risk of bias for individual RCTs using the modified Cochrane tool (RoB 2.0)" (supp/lrm-preox-40127663/mmc1.pdf, physical p11 (printed 10), Supplement 2 Protocol, 'Risks of bias')
- S2: "In terms of outcomes, we will include mortality (longest endpoint reported), peri-intubation hypoxemia (as defined by individual study authors), specific adverse events during pre-oxygenation or intubation [...], 1st pass success for intubation, and total serious adverse events" (supp/lrm-preox-40127663/mmc1.pdf, physical p11 (printed 10), Supplement 2 Protocol, 'Eligibility criteria')
- S3: "eTable 3. Risk of bias assessment for each reported outcome across each study" (supp/lrm-preox-40127663/mmc1.pdf, physical p26 (printed 25); table runs pp26-27; columns Study, Outcome, Domain 1-5, no overall column)
- S4: "We assessed the risk of bias using the modified Cochrane tool (RoB 2.0) and the certainty of evidence using the GRADE approach." (fetched/lrm-preox/epmc-abstract.json, abstract, Methods (Europe PMC record for PMID 40127663))
