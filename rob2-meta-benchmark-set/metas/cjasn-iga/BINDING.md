# cjasn-iga

Effects of therapies on proteinuria and eGFR in IgA nephropathy: MA of randomized trials
doi: 10.2215/cjn.0000000839

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: Each trial's treatment vs control (placebo, supportive care, or irbesartan for PROTECT). Outcomes extracted: % change in proteinuria from baseline (target 9 months, accepted 6-12 months, otherwise end of trial; S5-S7) and annual total eGFR slope (target 2 years, minimum 12 months for pooling). Multi-dose trials are entered per dose in Table 2: TESTING pooled plus reduced-dose and full-dose rows; ENVISION 2, 4 and 8 mg/kg IV rows (Table 1 prints '6 mg/kg', Table 2 prints 8mg/kg); Lv et al. 160 mg and 240 mg rows; ORIGIN atacicept 150 mg vs placebo. STOP-IgAN is described as 'versus placebo' in Tables 1-3 although the control arm was supportive care alone. The RoB table (S4) has one row per trial with no outcome named, so the ratings cannot be tied to proteinuria or eGFR from the meta's text; the meta's own outcomes are proteinuria change and eGFR slope.
