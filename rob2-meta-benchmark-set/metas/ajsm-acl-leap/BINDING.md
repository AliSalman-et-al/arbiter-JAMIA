# ajsm-acl-leap

Lateral extra-articular procedure added to ACL reconstruction: SR and MA of RCTs
doi: 10.1177/03635465241304781

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: All included trials are two-arm: primary ACLR plus a lateral extra-articular procedure (LET or ALL reconstruction) vs isolated primary ACLR. Pooled analyses used 6, 12 and 24 month time points (Figures 3-8); all else is narrative, with per-study outcomes and time points in Appendix 3 (sj-pdf-3). Reference numbers map to report: 7 Castoldi, 8 Chiba, 14 Getgood 2020 Arthroscopy (functional outcomes), 15 Getgood 2020 AJSM (failure, our getgood-2020), 16 Gibbs, 18 Hamido, 21 Heard, 24 Ibrahim. Figure 2 has no reference numbers, so the two 'Getgood et. al, 2020' rows are matched to reports only through A2/A3.

Statements in the meta about what the RoB 2 ratings cover:

- A1: "Structured into 5 domains of bias, the Risk of Bias 2 tool provides a framework for assessing the risk of bias in a single result from individual studies to provide a judgment (low, some concerns, or high)." (fulltext/PMC12381392.xml, Methods > Methodological Quality of Studies)
- A2: "The Cochrane risk-of-bias assessment for all 15 studies is shown in Figure 2 . Upon assessment, 10 studies were classified as low risk of bias, § 3 studies presented some concern, 15 , 56 , 59 and 2 studies were deemed to be of high risk of bias. 7 , 18" (fulltext/PMC12381392.xml, Results > Methodology Assessment)
- A3: "§ References 8 , 10 , 14 , 16 , 21 , 24 , 34 , 36 , 37 , 49 ." (fulltext/PMC12381392.xml, footnote to Results > Methodology Assessment (low-risk list))
- A4: "Four articles with some concern or high risk of bias 15 , 18 , 56 , 59 found a significant difference in outcome measures of interest; however, 8 articles 10 , 14 , 16 , 21 , 24 , 36 , 37 , 49 with low risk of bias also reported on these outcomes" (fulltext/PMC12381392.xml, Results > Methodology Assessment)
- A5: "Risk of Bias 2 assessment. D1, randomization process; D2, deviations from the intended interventions; D3, missing outcome data; D4, measurement of the outcome; D5, selection of the reported result." (fulltext/PMC12381392.xml (image: eval/meta-set/supp/PMC12381392/10.1177_03635465241304781-fig2.jpg), Figure 2 caption; one row per report, D1-D5 + Overall, no outcome column)
- A6: "Castoldi et al 7 noted an increase in the presence of OA in the lateral tibiofemoral compartment in the ACLR + LET group when compared with the ACLR-only group, although this study was of low quality." (fulltext/PMC12381392.xml, Results > Clinical Outcomes)
- A7: "Outcomes of interest: instability, stiffness, rerupture rates, pain, satisfaction, and physical function" (fulltext/PMC12381392.xml, Methods > Eligibility Criteria (PICOS))
