# jamapeds-pda

Active treatment vs expectant management of PDA in preterm infants: MA
doi: 10.1001/jamapediatrics.2025.1025

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: Two-arm comparison throughout: active treatment of PDA (pharmacologic, surgical ligation, percutaneous closure; in practice ibuprofen, indomethacin and/or paracetamol vs placebo or no treatment) vs expectant management, in the first 2 weeks of life. Outcomes are dichotomous, pooled as RR and RD, time point 36 weeks PMA or discharge. The lead pooled outcome (Figure 2A, post hoc) is death at 36 wk or at discharge (whichever later) or moderate to severe BPD. No outcome is named for the RoB assessment.
