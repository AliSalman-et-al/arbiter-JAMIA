# bja-prehab

Home-based prehabilitation: SR and MA of randomised trials
doi: 10.1016/j.bja.2025.01.010

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: Home-based prehabilitation vs standard care without prehabilitation (S10). For An 2021 (three arms) the meta used telerehabilitation (18) vs usual care (18); the patient-education arm (17) is not used (main Table 1 lists 36 patients). Ferreira 2021 and Gillis 2014 compare prehabilitation vs the same programme started after surgery (rehabilitation), entered as control. Complications have no time window; trial windows are 30 days (Gillis, Fulop), 90 days (Bausys, Jensen/Bente), or not stated for complications (Ferreira, postoperative Clavien grade). Effects are risk ratios (M-H, random) or mean differences; medians converted with Wan's method.
