# hru-preconception

Preconception lifestyle interventions for women: SR and MA
doi: 10.1093/humupd/dmaf021

Binding: one rating per trial, not tied to an outcome; it covers every outcome the meta took from that trial

Contrast: Lifestyle intervention (diet and/or physical activity; for Beerendonk 1996/1999 dietary sodium restriction) vs no intervention or standard minimal care (H6). Odds ratios for binary outcomes, mean differences for continuous outcomes, random effects. Time point: the latest reported in each trial (H4), though for Espinos 2017 the meta took live birth per initial cycle (11/21 vs 6/20), not the cumulative live birth rate (13/21 vs 6/20). Denominators: the meta used randomised numbers for Becker (16/19) and Einarsson (160/157) but per-protocol completers for Beerendonk 1999 (36/33). Weight pools change scores (Becker, Einarsson, Hanafiah) with a final value (Beerendonk 1996, weight on day of oocyte retrieval).
