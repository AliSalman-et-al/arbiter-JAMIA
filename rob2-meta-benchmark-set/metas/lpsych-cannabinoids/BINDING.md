# lpsych-cannabinoids

Cannabinoids for mental disorders and substance use disorders: SR and MA
doi: 10.1016/s2215-0366(26)00015-5

Binding: one rating per trial, not tied to an outcome; it covers every outcome the meta took from that trial

Contrast: Cannabinoid vs matching placebo (Appendix E comparator column). Multi-arm handling seen in the forest plots: Freeman 2020 400 mg and 800 mg each vs a split placebo (11/12) for continuous outcomes and abstinence, but combined arms (47 vs 23) for adverse events and dropouts. Herrmann 2016: zolpidem+nabilone vs placebo (the zolpidem-alone arm is not used; zolpidem counted as a pharmacological co-intervention, L9). Haney 2004: Study 1 only (oral THC 50 mg/day vs placebo during marijuana abstinence; the divalproex study is not a cannabinoid). Levin 2016: dronabinol+lofexidine vs placebo. Stated time point 'at longest follow-up' (L7), but Allsop's withdrawal value is the days 2-6 treatment-phase mean.
