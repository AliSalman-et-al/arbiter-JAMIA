# jacc-avr

AVR vs clinical surveillance in asymptomatic severe AS: SR and MA
doi: 10.1016/j.jacc.2024.11.006

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: Early AVR (SAVR or TAVR) vs clinical surveillance (CS), intention-to-treat HRs over each trial's full follow-up (study-level HRs pooled by random effects; average follow-up 4.1 years). No fixed time point.
