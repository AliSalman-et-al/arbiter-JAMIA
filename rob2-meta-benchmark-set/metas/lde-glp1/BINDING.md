# lde-glp1

GLP-1 RAs on kidney and CV outcomes: MA of RCTs
doi: 10.1016/s2213-8587(24)00271-7

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: GLP-1 receptor agonist vs placebo (all doses pooled within trial where trials had 2 doses, e.g. ELIXA, SUSTAIN-6, Harmony, AMPLITUDE-O, as per-arm 'GLP-1 RA' totals in the forest plots). Time-to-event HRs over the whole trial follow-up. Two main outcomes: a composite kidney outcome (L4) and 3-point MACE (L5); RoB is not tied to either.

- L1: "The risk of bias of each included study was assessed using the Cochrane Risk of Bias (RoB 2) assessment tool.29" (scratchpad/binding/fetched/lde-glp1/eprints_gla_342922.pdf (author submitted version), 8 (Methods))
- L2: "The risk of bias in each study is shown in Figure S2. All trials were assessed as low risk of bias in all domains." (scratchpad/binding/fetched/lde-glp1/eprints_gla_342922.pdf (author submitted version), 11 (Results))

- L4: "The main kidney disease outcome was a composite, consisting of kidney failure (kidney replacement therapy, persistent estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) <15 mL/min/1·73 m2), a sustained reduction in eGFR from baseline by ≥50%, or death due to kidney disease."
