# cid-rifampin

Safety of triple-dose rifampin in TB treatment: SR and MA
doi: 10.1093/cid/ciaf004

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: Primary analysis: TDR (>=30 mg/kg/d) arms vs SDR (10-14 mg/kg/d adults) arms, IRR of total SevAE events per person-year (Figure 2); arms meeting a dose class are pooled (S6), e.g. Boeree SDR = control + RIFQHZ arms. Secondary DDR-vs-SDR analyses (S8, Supplementary Figure 4A-C) pool all DDR arms vs SDR. No time point is stated for SevAE; person-years come from median follow-up x mITT (S7). Death and 2-month culture conversion are risk ratios on mITT denominators.
