# jama-sglt2

SGLT2 inhibitors and kidney outcomes: meta-analysis of large placebo-controlled trials (Neuen et al., JAMA 2025)
doi: 10.1001/jama.2025.20834

Binding: stated: rated for each trial's primary outcome (eTable 2 footnote)

Contrast: All trials: SGLT2 inhibitor vs placebo (eTable 2 Intervention/Comparator columns). RoB 2 was applied to each trial's own primary outcome (S1), which is NOT the outcome the meta analysed: the meta's outcomes are harmonised kidney outcomes (CKD progression primary, S4; eGFR slope, AKI), estimated per trial by Cox models 'as prespecified in each trial' (S5), ITT (S9). So the labels describe the trials' primary results, and our targets (trial primaries) are the right comparison. eTable 2 has five domain columns and no overall column; overall '(not given)' in TRIALS.csv is correct. No time point is pinned beyond each trial's follow-up. DELIVER: meta used the full randomised population (6263), not the LVEF <60% subset (S6, S7, S8).
