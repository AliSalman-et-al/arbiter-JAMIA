# lrheum-lbp

Long-term effectiveness of non-surgical interventions for chronic LBP: SR and MA
doi: 10.1016/s2665-9913(25)00064-5

Binding: stated: one rating per trial, covering pain intensity and disability at the time point closest to 12 months

Contrast: Intervention vs control comparator, where 'control' pools no intervention, placebo/sham, minimal intervention, adjuvant and usual care (S10); comparisons against another active intervention category are not meta-analysed. Multi-arm trials: arms of the same intervention category were combined (Abbasi: both multidisciplinary arms vs standard care, 19 vs 10) and control-type arms were combined (Ashar: PRT 50 vs placebo+usual care 101). Time point: closest to 12 months within 12 to <24 months (long-term) or closest to 3 years if >=2 years (very long-term); effects expressed as mean difference on a 0-100 scale (S14); same-category arms combined per S13.
