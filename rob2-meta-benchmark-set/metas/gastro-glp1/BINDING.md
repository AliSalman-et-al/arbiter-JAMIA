# gastro-glp1

GLP-1 RAs and GI adverse events: SR and MA
doi: 10.1053/j.gastro.2025.06.003

Binding: not stated by the meta; label_outcome is our inference from the meta's outcomes and analyses

Contrast: GLP-1RA (or dual agonist) vs placebo, risk ratio, one forest plot per GI event (Supplementary Figures 1-15, mmc1 pp46-60). For multi-arm trials the arm totals in the plots show: all GLP-1RA arms pooled against placebo (AWARD-1 both dulaglutide doses, exenatide arm dropped; AWARD-10 both dulaglutide doses; Frías all four tirzepatide doses plus the dulaglutide arm; GetGoal-F1 one-step plus two-step lixisenatide vs combined placebo). DURATION-8 has no placebo-only arm; the plots use exenatide+dapagliflozin (n=231) vs dapagliflozin+placebo (n=233), dropping exenatide+placebo. Time point is not stated anywhere we can see; the per-arm totals and event counts match the ClinicalTrials.gov results adverse-event tables (safety populations, registry time frames), not the papers, so the time point is the registry AE time frame, which for DURATION-8 (104 weeks) and GetGoal-F1 (up to 112 weeks) runs well past the primary endpoint. AWARD-1 numbers match the registry's 26-week groups, not its 56-week groups. The meta mixes registry serious and other (non-serious) AE tables between events and trials (see per-trial notes).
